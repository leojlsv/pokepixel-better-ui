# Code of conduct

This project expects technical discussion to remain respectful, specific and
focused on the work.

Contributors and maintainers should:

- critique code, behavior and evidence rather than people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and unwanted personal disclosure;
- keep bug reports and reviews reproducible and technically relevant;
- avoid publishing another person's private information or credentials;
- respect maintainer decisions on project scope, security and repository safety.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these rules and
may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its
participants.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel where possible. Do
not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
